# Branthaven IT Help – How to get help

Need IT? There are three ways to ask. They all create the same ticket, and you can follow it from start to finish.

## 1. Report an IT issue (recommended)

1. In Microsoft Teams, open **Report an IT issue** (in the left bar once IT has pinned it, or under Apps).
2. Fill in:
   - **What do you need help with?** A short summary, e.g. "Printer on 3rd floor won't print".
   - **Category** – this picks itself from what you type (e.g. "printer" gives Printer). Change it if it's wrong.
   - **How urgent is it?** *I can't work at all*, *It's slowing me down*, or *No rush*. Please be honest: it decides who gets help first.
   - **Details** – what happened and when it started. Error messages and the device name help a lot.
3. (Optional) **On behalf of** – if you are reporting this for a colleague, enter their Branthaven email address. The updates will go to them.
4. Click **Send to IT**. You will see **Ticket IT-<number> created**.

## 2. Email

Send an email to the IT help desk mailbox (IT will tell you the address). Put the problem in the subject and details in the body. A ticket is created from it.

## 3. The IT support form

Use the **IT Support** form link in the confirmation email or the Teams message. It asks the same questions as the app.

## What happens after you send it

1. **Confirmation** – you get an email and a Teams message from **IT Support** with your ticket number (IT-123).
2. **Updates** – whenever IT changes the status you are told by email and in Teams.
3. **Questions from IT** – if IT needs more information (status *Waiting for Client*), reply to the email or reply in the IT Support chat in Teams. Your reply goes straight to the ticket.
4. **Fixed?** – when IT thinks it's solved, the status becomes *Resolved Pending Confirmation*.
   - If it's fixed: nothing to do.
   - If it's **not** fixed: reply to the email or Teams message within a day.
   - You get a reminder after 24 hours. The ticket closes automatically 48 hours after it was resolved.

## Ticket statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Open | We've received your ticket |
| Assigned | A technician has been given your ticket |
| In Progress | IT has started working on it |
| Escalated or Waiting Internally | IT has asked another team for help |
| Waiting for Client | IT needs information from you |
| To Be Scheduled | IT wants to book a time with you |
| Resolved Pending Confirmation | IT believes it's fixed and is waiting for you to confirm |
| Closed | Done |

## See your tickets

Use **My tickets** (link in your emails) to see your own open tickets. You only ever see your own.

## Tips

- One problem per ticket. Use **Report a new issue** for a different problem.
- Emergency (nobody can work)? Choose *I can't work at all* and also call or message IT directly.
- Replies: keep the ticket number (e.g. [IT-123]) in the subject, and write above the line "## Reply above this line ##" if present.
